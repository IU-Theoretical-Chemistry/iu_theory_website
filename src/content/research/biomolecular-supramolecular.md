Fragment-based and machine-learning predictions of NMR chemical shifts, pKa values, and
protein–ligand binding affinities for structure-based drug design; quantum nuclear
effects in enzymatic hydrogen transfer; molecular recognition driven by noncovalent
interactions; and computer-aided design of macrocyclic anion receptors, foldamers, and
fluorescent supramolecular materials, often in collaboration with experimental groups
at IU.
