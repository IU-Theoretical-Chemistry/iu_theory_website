Includes quantum wavepacket ab initio molecular dynamics (QWAIMD), which propagates
quantum nuclei alongside electronic structure computed on the fly; ring-polymer and
surface-hopping methods that fold nuclear quantum effects into non-adiabatic dynamics;
rare-event sampling for quantum and classical ab initio dynamics; and finite-temperature
quantum dynamics for open systems in contact with an environment.
