The Molecules-in-Molecules (MIM) approach and its electrostatically embedded and
machine-learning variants, graph-theoretic fragmentation that leads naturally to tensor
network representations, connectivity-based hierarchies for systematic error
cancellation, and QM:QM embedding schemes such as ONIOM-CT and projection-based
wavefunction-in-DFT. A shared IU lineage: MIM was developed here during Mayhall's PhD
with Raghavachari.
