A program-wide strength. Adaptive variational algorithms — the ADAPT-VQE family — and
pulse-level quantum control tailor simulations of correlated electrons to the hardware
at hand; quantum nuclear dynamics has been mapped onto trapped-ion and spin-lattice
quantum simulators; theory for spin relaxation and decoherence guides the design of
solid-state and molecular qubits for sensing and communication; and materials discovery
supplies new platforms for quantum technology.
