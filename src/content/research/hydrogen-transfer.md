Applications include hydrogen tunneling in enzymes such as soybean lipoxygenase,
hydrogen-bonded clusters and radical chemistry in atmospheric and aerosol systems,
Grotthuss proton transport, the vibrational spectroscopy that probes hydrogen-bonded
networks, and electron and proton motion in molecular wires and at materials
interfaces.
