Spans first-principles exchange coupling constants in transition metal complexes via
spin-flip and tensor product methods, spin–phonon coupling and non-adiabatic spin
relaxation mechanisms that limit molecular qubit coherence, and collective magnetism in
extended systems — quantum spin chains, van der Waals magnets, and nonrelativistic spin
splitting in compensated magnets.
