Includes mechanistic studies of electrocatalytic CO₂ reduction and oxygen reduction at
molecular and carbon-based catalysts, non-adiabatic reaction dynamics at metal surfaces
and model battery interfaces, weak adsorbate–surface interactions computed at
coupled-cluster accuracy, and energy flow in condensed phases and at interfaces.
